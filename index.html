My job is to learn how you’re living your story right now, where you want to grow next, and what truly matters to you — then quietly shape what you notice in the world so that it all feels like a coherent journey, not just random moments.

🎨 Lenses: Explore / Serve / Create / Inspire / Reflect
📍 Layers: World / Community / Personal

Why this structure?

Whyld World exists to help people feel like superheroes in their own story, not just passive tourists. The structure is simple:

Chapters (Now & Next)
You choose the chapter you’re in (Exploring, Building, Healing, Transforming, Mastering, Leading, Living) and where you want to lean next. That gives Clara a story arc.
Meaning Drivers
You highlight what genuinely matters (family, creativity, nature, impact…). These become the gravity wells that bend what you notice.
Modes & Lenses
In Meaning Mode, Clara tunes your field. In Discovery Mode, AR overlays stories and reflections through different lenses and layers, so the physical world becomes a canvas for your inner journey.
