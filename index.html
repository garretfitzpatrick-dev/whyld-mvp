How this prototype works
Lenses · Layers · Locations
1
Set your arc here. Tell Whyld World what feels meaningful, which lens should lead, and how intense you want the signal. That becomes your temporary meaning profile.
2
Step into AR. On the next screen (ar.html), pointing your device at the marker or walking into a zone pops up a card. The content in that card is shaped by: lens (Explore / Serve / Create / Inspire / Reflect), layer (World / Community / Personal), and your meaning profile.
3
Feel your story against the world. World layer tells a place-anchored story, Community layer shows reflections from others, and Personal layer folds your arc, question, and “notice more of” into a short prompt aimed at your next small move.
In the full vision, this home view becomes your evolving Meaning Graph: trends over time, new arcs as your life shifts, and deeper ways of weaving in your reflections, quests, and collaborations.
