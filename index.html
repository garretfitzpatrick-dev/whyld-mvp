How Whyld World will feel on your face

When you tap Enter the World, the AR prototype uses this profile to subtly change what it shows you.

  • Lenses (Explore, Serve, Create, Inspire, Reflect) change how you look at the same place: curiosity, service, creativity, inspiration, or meaning-making.
  • Layers (World, Community, Personal) change who’s story you’re seeing: the place itself, the people around you, or your own inner arc.
  • Modes:
    • Discovery mode surfaces stories, history, quests and “wow, I never knew that” moments.
    • Meaning mode nudges you to connect those stories back to your chapter, your drivers, and your next small move.
