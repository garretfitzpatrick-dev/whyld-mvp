Why this structure?

Whyld World exists to help people feel like superheroes in their own story, active participants rather than spectators. The structure is simple and each part has a job:

Chapters (Story arc)
You choose the chapter you are in now (Exploring, Building, Healing, Transforming, Mastering, Leading, Living) and where you feel drawn next. That gives Clara a sense of your story arc: the act you are in and the direction you want to move.
Meaning drivers (Gravity)
You highlight what genuinely matters to you right now (for example: family, creativity, nature, health, impact). These become gravity wells: themes that pull more stories, prompts, and overlays into view when you move through the world.
Filters, goals & purpose
You set how strong you want the experience to feel, what you want to notice more, which life areas you are focused on, and a draft of why you are here. In Discovery Mode, Clara uses this to decide how often to nudge you, what kind of stories to surface first, and how moments connect toward your purpose.
Let Clara learn your story
This takes about a minute. You can change any of it later. I’ll quietly use it to shape the prompts and stories you see in Discovery Mode.
1. Which chapter are you in right now?
Pick the one that feels closest. This doesn’t lock you in — it just gives Clara a starting point.
2. Where do you feel drawn next?
Optional. If you skip this, Clara will just focus on deepening the chapter you’re already in.
3. What feels most meaningful right now?
Choose a few (three is a good starting point). These are the themes Clara will gently highlight in the world around you.
